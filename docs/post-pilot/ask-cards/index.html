Ask cards: one look for every confirm and every "go further" in the app

You said: "check the schedule maker's design and make everything in that card design where a user either needs to confirm or go further: haptic scrolls, rich design, scrollable big interactive pop-out questions and voice inputs where needed, not for this bit but for the entire app, without costing too much or rework." This page does exactly that as a design reference. Nothing is built.

What I took as "the schedule maker's design": the newest tappable schedule (the v5/v6 page at localhost:3075/v5.html): full-screen cards that snap one at a time with a tick, the question that rises as a big sheet with a giant हो and नाही (tap, or swipe the sheet right or left), the screen that will not move on until you answer, one continuous progress bar, and read-aloud. If you meant a different page, tell me which and I will redo this. Everything on this page works with your finger or mouse. The sample words and numbers on the phones are samples. Marathi that is not copied from the app is marked draft and needs your review. On a computer the buzz shows as a green glow and a line under the phone; open this page on an Android phone to feel it.

The short version

  1. Five kinds of card cover every confirm in the app: yes or no, pick one, a number, say it, and a stack of cards to go through. They all share one frame, so the look and the buzz are the same everywhere.
  2. It is cheap because the frame is one piece of code and the screens keep their logic. The app already has a buzz helper (shared/utils/haptics.ts) and a mic component; a screen adopts a card by changing what wraps its question, not what it saves. I counted the screens from the code in the map under decision 4.
  3. It is a design reference only. Your scope freeze (19 Nov) still stands; decision 5 says what could be built before 3 Dec and what should wait.

2How firmly a question holds the screen

In the Schedule Maker a question locks the scroll: you answer, then you go on. That is right when the answer changes what is saved (a dose, a "was it done"). It is wrong for a screen that only shows information, where a lock feels like a bug.

Switch "Hold the screen" on and off in section 1 and try kinds 1 and 5 both ways. On: the sheet shakes and thuds if you push past or tap the dark area. Off: tapping the dark area or swiping down puts the question away "for later" and the screen shows an amber ring on whatever is unanswered; the final save card still refuses until everything is answered.

Recommended A: hold only when the answer changes what is saved; information cards never hold. B: never hold, only the amber ring.

3Buzz and sound: one small set, used the same everywhere

Press "Feel it". On an Android phone each one buzzes with the pattern in the table; on a computer the phone glows and a sound plays. The set is small on purpose so a farmer learns it by body, not by reading: a short tick means "moved one step", a heavy knock means "not yet", two taps mean "yes", a long run means "saved".

NameWhenBuzz (milliseconds)Sound

Facts from the code: the app already has a buzz helper with five patterns (shared/utils/haptics.ts: light 10, medium 40, heavy 70, success 30-50-30, error 50-30-50-30-50) and the Capacitor haptics plugin is installed in package.json but not used anywhere yet. Today the buzz fires on tab taps, swipes and toggles; no screen that confirms or chooses buzzes. The sound is made on the phone with Web Audio (no files), as in the Schedule Maker page. Rules: never the only signal (the card always also shows a tick, a colour and words); a Profile switch for sound and for buzz; nothing plays when the phone is on silent.

Recommended A: buzz and sound both on by default, each with a switch in Profile. B: buzz on, sound off until switched on.

5What it costs, what is reused, and in what order

Reused (already in the app)

  • The buzz helper shared/utils/haptics.ts (five patterns). Today it fires on tab taps, swipes and toggles; no screen that confirms or chooses buzzes. Four new patterns are about ten lines.
  • The mic piece PlanMic plus understandPlanSpeech: about ten lines to put a mic on a card. The big mic screen (requestSpeak) saves a whole farm log, so it is not reused for a card answer.
  • The back-button helper useBackDismiss (32 files already use it), the plot selector from UI-9a, Tap and BigButton.

New (my estimate; the CTO sizes it before any build)

  • One frame (AskSheet): backdrop, rise, handle, the amber chip, ऐका, swipe down, the hold rule.
  • Four bodies (yes or no, pick one, number, say it), one stack with its one bar, one file for buzz and sound, one switch in Profile. About eight small files, none long.

Honest risks

  • Marathi words that are not copied from the app are drafts and need your review (हे निवडा, बोला, लिहून बदला, नंतर सांगतो, आजची नोंद तपासा).
  • Voice and privacy: the existing planning mic keeps the audio on the server for the AI job and a 30-day clip on the phone. There is also a device recogniser that keeps nothing but works only in the installed app (used on one screen today). A card mic should use the device recogniser first and fall back to the planning mic; that is a consent matter for you and the CTO to confirm before the voice cards ship.
  • Buzz exists only on Android. The app ships as an Android app (no iPhone plugin is installed), and a web page on an iPhone does not buzz. On a laptop you see a glow instead.
  • Holding the screen can trap a farmer if the question is unclear. So optional questions always have "नंतर", and only questions that change what is saved hold.
  • Two screens are protected by your own rules: the mic and waiting screens, and the voice result screen you chose on 21 Nov. They are not in this plan.

Your scope freeze (19 Nov: small changes only, test APK with real data by 3 Dec) still stands. This page is a reference, nothing is built, and nothing would start until you say so.

Order, smallest first

  1. Pilot: the frame, the buzz, the yes or no and the pick-one, on two screens (was the task done, why no work today). You feel it on the test phone, then decide. If you dislike it, two screens go back.
  2. Phase 1: the same two kinds on all 12 S rows.
  3. Phase 2, after 3 Dec: number, say-it and the stack on the 10 M rows, one screen per commit, daily ones first (check and save, labour approve).
  4. Later: the large bill flow, and the legal screens after your review.
Recommended A: the pilot (2 screens). B: phase 1 straight away (12 screens, K1 and K2 only). Anything bigger waits for the APK either way.
