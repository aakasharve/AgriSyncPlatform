The app asks. The farmer answers.

The BEFORE pictures are real screens from the test app, taken today. The AFTER phones are drawings that really work: touch them, tap the mic, drag the dial.

Voice stays the way the farmer talks. The Home voice flow is not touched. Only screens that need long reading or many taps turn into a conversation.

The idea in one minute

Today

The farmer meets a form. He reads the labels, finds the field, types, scrolls and taps save. Tabs and lists decide the order.

New

The app leads. It asks one plain question, he answers by voice or one tap, the app says what it understood, he confirms.

  • One question at a timeIn his own words: his name, his crop and his plot are inside the sentence.
  • Voice first, never voice onlyA big mic leads. Big one-tap answers and a typed way are always there too.
  • The app fills, he confirmsIt shows what it understood in plain words ("मला असं ऐकू आलं") and he taps one big "बरोबर".
  • A road, not tabsOne thin line shows the way and there is one big next. He can leave and come back to the same place.
  • Soft, never loudA slowly breathing mic, low sounds, a gentle buzz. No shake, no knock, no confetti, no red alarm.
  • Few blocks, reusedThe same ? blocks build all four conversations, and ? of them are used by more than one. A new screen costs a list of questions, not new design work.

The table is read from the working phones on this page, not typed by hand.

What does not change

  • The Home voice flow
    The "आज काय काम झालं" flow stays exactly as it is. It does not appear on this page.
  • The waiting screen and the result screen
    Not touched.
  • The schedule cards
    Another designer owns them.
  • The consent wording
    The notice and the tick sentence are the app's own words. Here they are only split into pages and read aloud.
  • The fields
    Every flow asks the same things today's forms ask. No field is added. Anything new is marked "new behaviour" next to the phone.

1How it should feel

Six small effects carry the whole feeling. Press each one. The sounds are low sine tones (gain 0.05 to 0.08, fading in 300 to 700 ms); the buzz patterns are very short. Buzz works on Android only, because the app ships as an Android app; on a computer you see a soft glow instead.

2The setup path: a road, not a list

What is wrong today. The setup hub is one long list: five setup rows over 3 phone screens that all look the same, each with a "पूर्ण" chip and a "?" button, and a counter that says "4 पैकी 4 पूर्ण". Nothing leads the farmer to the next job.
