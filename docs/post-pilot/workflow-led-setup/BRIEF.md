# Brief: workflow-led setup, real BEFORE screens and operating AFTER screens

You are a senior mobile product designer. Build ONE design-reference web page for the founder of ShramSafal (a voice-first farm-record app for smallholder grape and sugarcane farmers in Maharashtra; farmers read Marathi, many with low literacy). The page shows REAL BEFORE screens of the current app next to AFTER screens that the reader can OPERATE, and lets the founder approve each one or write a change. You build nothing in the app's repo. You only write files in this folder.

Load the `frontend-design:frontend-design` skill first and follow its process (short design plan, review against this brief, build, critique with screenshots). The brief's own words win over its defaults.

## The founder's words (verbatim, he is not a native English writer)
"I think you are going in wrong direction. The voice is the only way that we are going to interact, but the आज काय काम झालं is need to be as it is. All those things are as it is. The current UI is appropriate but still follows the old way of interaction. We should make it more personal, more bigger text, more operative driven, like workflow driven. App is just like the tabs are the only way that being operated. Everything is there asked for him as like that."

My reading, which you must follow:
1. Voice stays THE way the farmer talks to the app. The Home "आज काय काम झालं" voice flow, the waiting screen and the result screen are NOT touched and do not appear in this page.
2. Only screens that are static, need long reading, or need many taps/fields become interactive. Today's examples are in the real BEFORE pictures: the setup hub, sign-in and consent notice, water and power, crops and plots, inviting someone.
3. The new idea: the app LEADS, the farmer ANSWERS. One plain question at a time, in his own words (his name, farm, crop and plot names inside the sentence), big text, a big mic first and big one-tap answers under it, a typed or tapped way always available (voice first, never voice only). The app fills the form from what he said, shows what it understood in plain words, and he confirms with one big tap. A visible road (workflow), always one big "next", resume where you left, instead of tabs and long forms.
4. Soothing: slow, soft, warm. Gentle buzz, soft low sounds, calm colours, breathing mic, no shake, no knock, no confetti, no red alarms. It must feel like a calm person asking, not a game.
5. Must not cost much to build later: reuse the same few question types everywhere, so say so on the page.

## Hard rules (project rules, non-negotiable)
- No emoji anywhere. Icons are inline SVG only.
- Marathi screens use Marathi only (Devanagari digits). English only on the page's own explanation text around the phones.
- No fake numbers and no invented facts. Sample names/numbers on AFTER phones are labelled "sample". The mic in demos is a DRAWING: it plays a fixed sample sentence; label it "demo voice, sample words".
- No "AI" wording on any phone screen. Say "मला असं ऐकू आलं" (what I heard).
- Voice fills ONLY the one thing asked. Money is never shown or asked in these flows.
- Consent wording is the app's own text, verbatim (read it from `src/clients/mobile-web/src/features/consent/gate/consentNotice.ts`). You may split it into pages and read it aloud; you may not reword it.
- Do not invent fields the app does not have. If your AFTER needs new behaviour the app lacks (for example adding several plots in one go), keep it out of the demo or mark it clearly on the page as "new behaviour, not in the app today".
- Plot names are the farmer's own. The app may only SUGGEST "variety + number".
- Light theme only. Minimum text on phones: question 30 px, answers 22 px, helper lines 17 px, nothing under 15 px. Tap targets at least 64 px tall for main answers.
- Page must work at 1200 px and at 390 px width with no horizontal scroll, keyboard focus visible, reduced motion respected.
- Do not touch anything outside this folder. No git. No repo edits. Do not leave any server running when you finish.

### The operating phones: one shared engine, five flows
Common to every flow: a thin calm progress line (one continuous line, no "1/7" counters), Sathi's small avatar speaking the question (use `img/a-sathi-point.png` or `a-sathi-welcome.png` sparingly), the question in 30 px bold with the farmer's own words in it, then ONE hero: a big breathing mic (slow 5 s breathing, not rings), then big answer tiles or a big number or a big dial, then a quiet "लिहा" / "निवडा" link for typing or tapping instead. After a voice answer: a card "मला असं ऐकू आलं: ..." with one big "बरोबर" and a quiet "पुन्हा बोला". After each step a soft 600 ms ease transition. Back is always available. Leaving and resuming is shown once (the path remembers where you were). Ending of a flow: a soft "छान" line, what is now ready in plain words, and the next stop on the path. Use the app's colours: emerald #047857 / #065F46, mint #ECFDF5, stone neutrals, warm cream #FAF7F0 is allowed as the calm background; amber #B45309 only for "needs your attention". The app's fonts are DM Sans and Noto Sans Devanagari; use "Noto Sans Devanagari" with "Nirmala UI" and system fallbacks.

### Test hooks (required, harmless)

Steps must be numbered 1..N per flow and N documented in NOTES.md. Also expose `window.__flows = {path:N, signin:N, ...}`.
