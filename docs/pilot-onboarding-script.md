# Pilot onboarding script — read this aloud to every pilot farmer

<!-- spec: dfes-companion-2026-07-11 (wave-4.3) -->

> **Founder-authored draft. Not reviewed by a lawyer.**
> This is a speaking script, not a legal instrument. It exists so that the same eight
> things get said to every pilot farmer, in his language, out loud — because a consent
> screen he taps through is not the same as a person telling him what happens to his
> voice. It does not replace the Terms of Use or the Privacy Notice; it is the spoken
> half of them.

**Who reads it:** the founder, face to face, before the farmer taps *सहमत आहे आणि पुढे जा*.
**How long:** about three minutes. Do not shorten beat 3.
**Every claim below was checked against the code on 2026-08-17.** If the app changes, this
script changes with it — see "When this script goes stale" at the end.

---

## The script

Speak the **Marathi**. The English underneath is for the founder's own check, not to be
read out.

### 1. What this is

> **मराठी —** "हे ॲप अजून तयार होतंय. तुम्ही पहिल्या काही शेतकऱ्यांपैकी एक आहात. ते फुकट आहे,
> आणि ते बंद पडेल, चुका करेल — तसं झालं तर मला सांगा. तीच तर मदत मला हवी आहे."

*EN — This app is still being built. You are one of the first few farmers. It is free, it
will break and it will make mistakes. Tell me when it does — that is the help I need.*

### 2. What it records

> **मराठी —** "तुम्ही बोललात की ॲप ते ऐकतं आणि दिवसाचं काम लिहून ठेवतं — शेत, पीक, कामं, मजुरी,
> खर्च. माईक तुम्ही सुरू केल्यावरच चालतो. तुमच्या फोनचा सतत मागोवा घेतला जात नाही."

*EN — When you speak, the app listens and writes down the day's work: farm, crop, tasks,
wages, costs. The microphone runs only when you start it. Your phone is not continuously
tracked.*

### 3. His workers' names — **do not skip, do not paraphrase**

> **मराठी —** "एक गोष्ट स्पष्ट सांगतो. **तुम्ही बोलताना तुमच्या मजुरांची आणि मुकादमांची जी नावं
> घेता, ती नावं ॲपमध्ये साठवली जातात** — कोणत्या शेतावर, किती वेळा, काय ठरलं, किती दिले, हेही.
> तुम्ही फॉर्ममध्ये नाव टाकायची गरज नाही; ॲप ते तुमच्या बोलण्यातूनच घेतं.
>
> त्या माणसांनी कुठल्याही स्क्रीनवर होकार दिलेला नाही. तुम्ही दिला आहे. **म्हणून तुमच्या
> माणसांना हे सांगा — 'माझ्या ॲपमध्ये तुझं नाव आणि तुझं काम लिहिलं जातं.'** ते मी तुमच्यावतीने
> करू शकत नाही. ते तुम्हीच करायचं आहे."

*EN — One thing I want to be clear about. **The names of the workers and mukadams you
mention are stored in the app** — which farm, how many times, what was agreed, what was
paid. You do not type them into a form; the app takes them from what you said. Those
people did not agree on any screen. You did. So please tell them: "your name and your work
are recorded in my app." I cannot do that for you.*

### 4. Where his voice goes

> **मराठी —** "तुमचा आवाज समजून घ्यायला बाहेरच्या कंपन्यांची मदत घेतो. बहुतेक वेळा ते भारतातच
> होतं — मुंबईच्या सर्व्हरवर, आणि Sarvam नावाच्या भारतीय कंपनीकडे. पण ती नीट समजू शकली नाही, तर
> **तोच आवाज Google कडे जातो, आणि तो भारताबाहेर जाऊ शकतो.** फक्त मजकूर परत येतो.
> हवामानासाठी शेताची जागा Tomorrow.io नावाच्या अमेरिकन कंपनीकडे जाते. OTP साठी तुमचा नंबर
> MSG91 कडे जातो."

*EN — We use outside companies to understand your voice. Most of the time it stays in
India — servers in Mumbai, and an Indian company called Sarvam. But if it cannot
understand the recording, **the recording itself goes to Google, and that may be processed
outside India.** Only text comes back. Weather: your farm's location goes to Tomorrow.io,
a US company. OTP: your number goes to MSG91.*

### 5. What we will never do

> **मराठी —** "तुमची माहिती विकणार नाही. जाहिरातींसाठी वापरणार नाही. आणि **तुमचा आवाज AI
> शिकवण्यासाठी कधीच वापरणार नाही** — परवानगी घेऊनही नाही. ॲपमध्ये तसा पर्यायच नाही.
> तुमच्या माहितीवरून कर्ज किंवा विम्याचे निर्णय घेणार नाही."

*EN — We will not sell your data, not use it for advertising, and **never train AI on your
voice** — not even with permission; there is no such switch in the app. No lending or
insurance decisions from your data.*

### 6. How long it is kept — say the awkward part

> **मराठी —** "फोनवरचं रेकॉर्डिंग ३० दिवसांत आपोआप जातं. पण **आमच्या सर्व्हरवर ठेवलेल्या
> रेकॉर्डिंगला आपोआप नष्ट होण्याची तारीख नाही.** ते तुमचं खातं चालू असेपर्यंत राहतं. तुम्ही
> सांगितलंत, तर आम्ही ते हातानं काढून टाकतो. मी हे लपवत नाही — तुम्ही सांगेपर्यंत ते तिथेच आहे."

*EN — The recording on your phone goes automatically after 30 days. But **the recording
kept on our servers has no automatic deletion date.** It stays while your account is open.
If you ask, a person at ARVE deletes it by hand. I am not hiding this — until you ask, it
sits there.*

### 7. Age, and stopping

> **मराठी —** "हे ॲप १८ वर्षांखालील कुणासाठी नाही. आणि कधीही थांबता येतं — सांगा, आणि खातं आणि
> माहिती काढून टाकतो."

*EN — Not for anyone under 18. You can stop any time — say so and we remove the account
and the data.*

### 8. Who to call

> **मराठी —** "काहीही अडलं, किंवा माहिती काढायची असेल, तर मला थेट फोन करा. arvesystems@gmail.com
> वरही लिहू शकता. कंपनीचा वेगळा पत्ता अजून केलेला नाही — तो माझाच ईमेल आहे, हे मी स्पष्ट सांगतो."

*EN — Anything stuck, or you want data removed: call me directly, or write to
arvesystems@gmail.com. There is no separate company mailbox yet — that is my own email
address, and I am saying so plainly.*

## What NOT to say

These are the four sentences that would turn an honest onboarding into a false one.

1. **Do not say the app is "DPDP compliant" or "legally approved."** It is not, and no
   document in this repo claims it. The Terms and the Privacy Notice both say on their
   face that they are founder-authored and not counsel-reviewed.
2. **Do not promise the numbers are right.** The app does not invent figures — every
   quantity comes from what he said — but speech understanding is imperfect. Tell him to
   read what the app understood before confirming it.
3. **Do not say his workers have agreed to anything.** They have not. Whether he may
   lawfully provide their details at all is an open question with a lawyer.
4. **Do not promise a deletion the software does not perform.** Erasure today removes his
   account, his Voice Diary and his personal details and anonymises his farm records. It
   does **not** currently reach the raw voice files in beat 6 — those are removed by hand.

## When this script goes stale

Re-read it against the code, and change it, on any of these:

- A new integration lands under `ShramSafal.Infrastructure/Integrations/**` or a new
  outside service is called from the client → **beat 4** gains a company.
- `src/clients/mobile-web/src/domain/privacy/RetentionPolicy.ts` changes a number or a
  mechanism → **beat 6** changes with it.
- Anything makes `UserConsentState.VerbatimTrainingCorpus` reachable from a screen or an
  endpoint → **beat 5 stops being true** and must be rewritten before the next farmer.
- The raw-voice bucket gains an S3 lifecycle `Expiration` rule → **beat 6** gains a date.
- A company mailbox is configured → **beat 8** loses its apology.

The written half of all this is
`src/clients/mobile-web/public/legal/{terms,privacy}_{mr,en}.md`. If this script and those
documents ever disagree, the documents are what he accepted — fix the script.
