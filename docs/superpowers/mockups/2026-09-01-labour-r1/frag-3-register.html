३ · The हजेरी वही — the register that remembers

This is the book the farm already keeps, drawn on a phone. It does not interview the farmer and it does not do sums for him. It reads back what was said — and it is careful about the difference between something that was said and something nobody has said yet. Everything below sits in the ledger that is already built and already liked: same box, same colours, same size. What is new is what that box is now allowed to hold.

The one test

“Where is ShramSafal asking me for something I already told it?”

For this page the answer is: nowhere. The हजेरी वही only shows back. It never re-asks a plot, a crop, a count or a name that was already given.

An empty box and a dash are two different facts

–नाही

A statement. Somebody looked and said he was not there.

 

Silence. Nobody has said anything about that day.

Not knowing a man was away is not the same as knowing he was away. Putting a dash where nobody spoke would be ShramSafal inventing an absence, and that invented absence is the one thing this release exists to remove. So an empty box stays empty, and a week nobody spoke about ends in —, never 0.

3.2  The register is never blocked

A book with nothing written in it is still a book. The हजेरी वही opens with no work written down for the day, with no headcount given, with not a single mark on the page, with nobody added to the farm yet, and on a day the farmer himself said no work happened. In every one of those the week is drawn and the days are drawn — because the page has to be there for him to write into.

One box, not four. The day fact sits on top and the second fact sits under it, so a day never has to be traded away to record an hour.

Hours are a length of time, never a time of day

3 तास means three hours long. It never means three o’clock. There is no start time, no finish time and no clock anywhere in हजेरी. When the pump ran at eleven at night because the power was on, that belongs to the work log — not to the attendance book.

So “गणेश रात्री 3 तास होता” is two facts, and both are kept:

The एकूण line counts named people only, so a crew’s number never joins it. When 8 go to the grapes and 4 to the banana on the same day, both are drawn and neither is added — 12 is printed nowhere.

Where money sits today, and what it is allowed to do

  • •  An ordinary day carries attendance marks and nothing else.
  • •  A day paid differently from the usual rate carries that amount inside that day.
  • •  The end of a person’s row carries the week’s money — only where a figure was actually stated for that person.
  • •  Where nothing was said about money, the row ends in —, never ₹0. Zero is a claim; a dash is honest.

Every rupee shown is a rupee the farmer said. ShramSafal counts marks; it never works out money. It does not multiply days by a rate, and it does not add two stated amounts together — which is why the bottom money line is a dash even though ₹4,650 and ₹450 are both on the page. No week figure was ever spoken, so there isn’t one.
