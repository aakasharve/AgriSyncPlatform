## D6 — one Labour, two money truths

- Labour home shows **two separate cards**: `रोजंदारी · नोंदलेली ₹…` and
  `उक्ते काम · ठरलेली ₹…`. **Never one combined figure** — his rule verbatim: the system
  never says "₹16,650 खर्च".
- Aggregation boundary, stated precisely: summing SAME-KIND stated amounts under an honest
  label (नोंदलेली / ठरलेली) is display of what was recorded. Forbidden remain: blending the
  two kinds, rate × days derivation, and presenting agreed as spent. Actually-paid money
  appears only as **दिलेली रक्कम** when a payment is recorded (advance rules: record yes,
  transact no).
- Headcount line: "आज कामावर 12 जण — 4 रोजंदारी · 8 उक्ते" — attendance counts stay whole;
  the arrangement is a breakdown, not a filter of who was present.
- Contract card (उक्ते काम tile): काम · प्लॉट · कोणाला · किती · रक्कम · कधीपर्यंत, plus a
  progress line — **progress is farmer-STATED ("काम अंदाजे ¾ पूर्ण"), never computed.**
- His scope fence, verbatim in effect: this design finalises the *direction*; the full
  contract lifecycle (measurement rules, milestones, payouts, worker distribution) grows in
  a later phase, and the current build must be shaped so adding it never remodels
  Labour/हजेरी.
