Where the arithmetic came from. All four of your strings are drawn verbatim — but “12 पैकी 12” needs twelve people on screen, and Ganesh + Ramesh + Shankar himself + the 8 with him is eleven. So one more named worker (Ashok) is drawn, rather than printing that line over eleven people. Shankar's own presence stays his own row — the 8 are never folded into his tick, and they are never drawn as eight rows. And why the reading is printed, not assumed. “शंकर आठ जण घेऊन आला” is two-way in Marathi. Spec D9.5 forbids settling it by undocumented assumption and says confirmation settles it — so his words and the reading sit together, and the बरोबर / बदल करा pair already on this frame is what decides it. What this screen must never do

Check the rule as well as the picture. Every line below is a constraint on State A, not a preference.

  • No Work Log bucket. No crop activity, irrigation, inputs, machinery or expenses anywhere in this flow. This is Labour's own surface; if a Work Log bucket appears, the farmer has been handed back to the generic log.

  • No crop or plot picker. द्राक्ष-२ appears only as a carried fact with no chevron, no tap target and no “change” affordance. He already said where he was working. The recorder's “select a crop or plot first” lock must never fire here.

  • No generic तपासा inbox card. The hub's review tile is deliberately absent from frame 1. A confirmation queue is a different job from taking today's हजेरी.

  • No headcount stepper, and no count entry of any kind. No +/− control, no number pad, no “how many came?”. The 12 is printed as something he already told the app. Asking for it again is the exact drift this screen is the test for.

  • No amount. No ₹ figure, no rate, no wage, no total — not on the hero, not on the result, not in the हजेरी. This flow answers who; money lives in the register. The hub's people list is omitted from frame 1 for exactly this reason.

  • No auto-save, and no “saved” state before बरोबर. Frame 3 carries no saved chip, no toast, no spinner that implies a write. बरोबर is the only save event in this flow; the confirmation in frame 4 exists only because he pressed it.

  • No clock times. Attendance remembers duration, never a range — no 7:00–11:00, no from/to fields, no time picker. Operational timing belongs to the Work Log.

  • No fabricated absence, and no invented people. A day nobody spoke about is a blank dashed box, never –. The 8 who came with Shankar are one line, never eight rows with made-up names, and never merged into Shankar's own tick.
