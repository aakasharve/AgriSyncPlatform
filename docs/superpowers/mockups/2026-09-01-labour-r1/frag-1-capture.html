१ · Recording who came — the capture journey

This is one journey, not four screens. The farmer speaks his day into the main log the way he always has. The app understands from that sentence that 12 people worked on द्राक्ष-२ today. Labour then asks him the one thing that sentence did not contain — who those twelve were. He answers out loud. ShramSafal shows him its own answer, he taps बरोबर, and the day is recorded. That is the entire loop.

Everything below is drawn, not running. The names, dates and numbers are sample content.

ShramSafal shows its own answer and waits. Shankar's own presence is his own line; the eight who came with him are one fact, never eight invented names. One tap on बरोबर records the day — that tap is the only moment anything is saved.

Why his own sentence is printed above ShramSafal's reading. “शंकर आठ जण घेऊन आला” can be heard two ways — Shankar plus eight, or eight including him. Rather than guess quietly or stop him with an extra question, the app shows both his words and the reading it took, together, inside the same confirm. बरोबर accepts that reading; बदल करा changes it. The four named people plus the eight with Shankar are the 12 the work log first reported.

What to check across all six screens

Count the questions. Across the whole journey the app asks exactly one thing — या 12 जणांमध्ये कोण होते? — and, only when two of his own statements disagree, one more to settle a single person's day. The date, the plot, the crop, the headcount and every name he already spoke are printed back to him as facts, never as fields. There is no plot chooser, no counter, no clock, no form, and no amount of money anywhere in this journey.
