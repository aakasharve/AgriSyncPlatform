One control — Allow Labour Management

The rule this screen exists to hold

मुकादम = who this person is on the farm.

Allow Labour Management = whether the owner trusts him with this responsibility.

Two different facts. They are never the same switch, and turning one off never touches the other. The owner's three answers are ON, ON until a date, and OFF — and once it is ON the Mukadam acts on his own. The owner is not asked again.

The test every screen must pass: “Where is ShramSafal asking me for something I already told it?” This screen asks for exactly one thing the owner has never said — whether he trusts this man, and until when. It asks for no plot, no crop, no count, no name.

What this screen must never do

The rule, so it can be checked as well as the picture.

  1. Never a matrix. No grid of people against things they may do. One man, one line, one switch.
  2. Never separate switches for attendance, expenses and approvals. There is one thing to allow, not three. Splitting it invents a system the farm does not have.
  3. Never the system's vocabulary. Not role, claim, policy, grant, ACL, permission model, membership, or RBAC — in any language, anywhere on the screen. It says who he is on the farm, and what the owner has allowed.
  4. Never a second screen for “until Sunday”. No wizard, no dialog, no separate temporary flow. The duration attaches inside the same row, to the same switch — frames 2 and 3.
  5. Never per-action owner approval. Once it is ON the Mukadam acts on his own. An owner who has to approve each attendance entry was never given this control — he was given more work.
  6. Never confuse who he is with what he may do. Turning this OFF must never remove the मुकादम badge, and being मुकादम must never turn it ON by itself. Frame 1, row 2 is the whole point of the screen.
  7. Never rewrite the past when authority ends. An ending denies the next action. It must never blank, re-attribute or hide a day already recorded — प्रकाश's three days keep his name and his face forever.
  8. Never backfill anyone to ON. Existing Mukadams start OFF at migration and stay OFF until the owner says otherwise. No “we kept it as it was for you”.
  9. Never ask for something already told. No plot, no crop, no headcount, no name is requested here. The only inputs are the trust decision and, optionally, the day it ends — the two things only the owner knows.
  10. Never pretend the Marathi exists. “Allow Labour Management”, “ON” and “OFF” ship in English today because no Marathi was ever approved. Until it is, they stay marked as an open copy item — they are not quietly translated by an agent.
