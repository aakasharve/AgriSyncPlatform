What this must never turn into

  • Never a grid of people against things they may do. One man, one line, one switch.
  • Never three switches for attendance, money and approvals. There is one thing to allow, not three.
  • Never a separate screen for “until Sunday”. The day it ends belongs inside the same switch.
  • Never make you approve each day. ON means he gets on with it — you keep the book, not a queue of permissions to grant.

Marathi for Allow Labour Management, ON and OFF has never been approved, so those three stand in English here and stay on your copy list. No agent invents those words.
