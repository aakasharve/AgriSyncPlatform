## Governing principle (founder, 2026-08-16)

> The safest design is: **one simple visual acceptance button, but two separate legal records behind
> it** — one for Terms acceptance and one for DPDP consent. **Optional purposes must remain separate.**
>
> A blanket "Accept everything now and forever" consent is invalid because DPDP consent must be
> specific, informed, purpose-limited and withdrawable as easily as it was given. The final Rules also
> require an itemised description of personal data and the purpose of each use.

Sources: [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) ·
[DPDP Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

**Founder's stated intent:** one calm, simple opening page where the farmer understands what
information ShramSafal uses, why each type is needed, who may process it, what ShramSafal will not do,
and how he can control, export or delete it — completing the entire legal acceptance through one clear
action, while the system separately preserves proof of Terms acceptance and DPDP consent.

## ENGLISH — final screen copy (verbatim)

Before you begin

Your farm data stays under your control.

ShramSafal uses only the following information to provide its services:

**Your account**
Mobile number, name and account information—for OTP login, account access and security.

**Your farm work**
Farm, plot, crop, work, labour, cost, income and team information—to understand your daily work, organise it and show clear summaries.

**Your voice and uploaded information**
Information you speak, type, photograph or upload—to understand your work and place it in the correct sections. The microphone is used only when you start recording.

**Farm location**
Your farm location or boundary—to provide weather and plot-specific context. ShramSafal will not continuously track the movement of your phone.

**Technical information**
Limited device, app, sync and security information—to protect offline work, synchronise it later, prevent misuse and diagnose technical failures.

**What we will not do**

• We will not sell your personal data.
• We will not use it for advertising without separate permission.
• We will not use your voice to train AI models without separate permission.
• We will not use your information to make lending, insurance or market-related decisions without asking for separate, specific consent.

Necessary and trusted service providers may process limited information for OTP delivery, secure cloud hosting, speech processing, weather, file storage, notifications and technical support. They may use it only for the assigned purpose.

**Your rights**

From Settings → Data & Privacy, you can:

• Review or withdraw your consent.
• Access, correct, complete, update or download your information.
• Request deletion of your account and personal data.
• Raise a grievance or nominate someone to exercise your rights.

Withdrawing essential consent may disable some or all ShramSafal services. It will not invalidate processing already lawfully completed. Limited information may still be retained where required by law.
