Shram Safal is a Daily Farm Execution System. Farmers keep their farm's record by speaking or tapping, and it becomes the farm's trusted memory. Once a farmer allows it, the FPO or FPC will see a combined picture of those farms: work, schedule changes, spray rules, tests and labour. This is being developed with the first partners.
