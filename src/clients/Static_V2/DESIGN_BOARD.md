# Static_V2 Illustrator Design Board

Purpose: make the homepage feel like a cinematic Marathi product film for farm discipline, not a feature-led SaaS page.

## Visual Rule

- Use real/generated photographic images for inspectable human, field, soil, phone, and farm atmosphere moments.
- Use HTML/CSS/SVG for proof systems, motion graphics, route cues, logo reveal, discipline spines, grain particles, topology lines, and UI fragments.
- Keep generated images text-free and logo-free. Real Marathi copy, ShramSafal logo, proof UI, and form fields are rendered in code.

## Scene 06 Logo Reveal Direction

Scene 6 is the first real brand arrival. It should feel like ShramSafal is forming from daily farm work:

1. The generated phone/field image creates the physical world.
2. The transparent logo mark and wordmark sit inside an orbital glass lockup.
3. Voice stays abstract as a waveform only; do not reveal a voice-log phrase.
4. Work chips attach to the proof spine: काम, मजुरी, खर्च, वेळ, निरीक्षण, जबाबदारी, पुरावा.
5. Proof nodes explain the system: Voice, Structure, Proof, Memory.
6. The section lands on: "आजचं काम उद्याच्या निर्णयात बदलतं."
