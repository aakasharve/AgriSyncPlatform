Delete your Shram Safal account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and your personal data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — inside the app

  1. Open the Shram Safal app and sign in.
  2. Open your Profile page.
  3. Tap Erase my data (डेटा पुसा) and follow the steps to request deletion of your account and data.

Option 2 — by email, if you can no longer use the app

Write to arvesystems@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Shram Safal account, and include the mobile number you signed in with so we can find your account. We may reply to confirm it is really you before we act.

What is deleted

  • Your account.
  • Your Voice Diary recordings.
  • Your personal details, including your name and personal notes.

What is kept, and why

  • Farm work records (logs, labour, costs) are kept, but with you removed from them. The figures stay; your name does not.
  • Voice recordings kept on our servers as evidence behind a saved record are not removed automatically by the deletion request today. We remove them by hand when you ask, so please mention them in your request.
  • Database backups are kept for up to 607 days (about 20 months) and are then deleted automatically.
  • Consent and audit records — the record of what you were shown and what you agreed to — are kept for the life of the service, and the app is technically prevented from editing or deleting them.
We have not set a fixed number of days for completing a deletion, and we would rather say so than promise one we cannot show. We will confirm to you when it is done.
